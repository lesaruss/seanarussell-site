Part three · The interview

Questions from the desk.

What an attorney or manager usually asks first, answered up front.

Who is the author of the music?
Sean. He writes the lyrics, beatboxes the beat and produces. AI tools are the studio he works in, not the author of the work.
Is there a track record, or is this new?
Thirty years. Anime 3000 passed one million podcast downloads, Top Spot USA put out 181 videos with 116 artists, and he has worked with or alongside MTV, ABC, NBC and Nintendo.
What kind of arrangement is he open to?
For an attorney, a percentage arrangement on deals is welcome. The ideal partner is AI-literate and opens doors.
Why a manager now?
He has always built the capability first and let the business lag. LESARUSS is the first time he is building the business with the community, and one career needs one steady hand across many projects.
What is GeekFon Society, in one line?
An animated music universe, begun March 2026, with 121 songs live across a roster of animated artists, a daily group-chat story, membership, $11 albums and radio.
What happens after I reach out?
Your note goes straight to Sean, not to a general inbox. If you are making an introduction, say who and why, and he will take it from there.
