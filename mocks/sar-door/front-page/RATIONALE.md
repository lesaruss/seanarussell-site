# front-page: the cover story

**Big idea.** Sean is the cover subject of his own magazine. The wordmark *is* the masthead ("Sean A. Russell." in Montserrat 900 across the full width, orange period kept), with a dateline (Vol. 30, No. 1, The Founder Issue), cover lines that are the four live-proof numbers, an "Exclusive" authorship strip and a barcode. Below the fold it reads like an issue: an "Inside" contents page with page numbers, a long-form cover story in newspaper columns, a "For the Record" spread for attorneys, "The Universe" as four titles from one publisher, "The Archive" as back issues, and a dark "Subscriber Edition" for the showcase. The representation page is a broadsheet Special Report: "What he has" ledger, "What he wants" classified ads, an on-the-record band, a Q&A interview, and "Letters to the desk" (3-step form).

**How the asks read.** Each audience gets one door, everywhere: Representation, Investors (one brand or the universe), Unlock the showcase (ink). They repeat on the cover, the "Three ways in" spread and the footer. Media kits, Press, Case studies and Book move to a footer "Media room".
