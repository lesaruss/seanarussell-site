Liner notes

The capability always came first.

At 14, in 1996, I started building paid websites for nonprofits, family and local businesses. By 1997 I was running an online writing federation with two full shows a week. In a 10th grade logo assignment that same year, I named a company LESARUSS.

That has been the pattern for thirty years. I built the capability first: the anime site and its podcasts, the music media outlet, the student newsroom, the district websites. The business always lagged behind the work.

"I've always been a Community Builder, even before having the language for what I was doing."Sean A. Russell
PAGE 02

LESARUSS is the first time I am building the business with the community, not after it. The tools finally caught up with the way I have always worked. One person can now write, produce and ship at the speed of an idea.
