# Liner notes: rationale

## The big idea
Sean's career, packaged like a record. The front door is the album: a vinyl sleeve with his photo, an obi strip, a "121 songs live" sticker on the shrink-wrap, and a record sliding out. Below it, sections follow how you read a record: pressing info (live proof), Side A (the four brands as a tracklist, GeekFon Society as A1), Credits ("I wrote it. I produced it. I built it." with Lyrics, Beat, Producer and AI tools listed as the Studio), Liner notes (the pattern, set as a two-page booklet spread), Side B (the discography, 1996 to now), a Thank you line (organizations), Bonus tracks (the asks) and a Hidden track (the showcase). The representation page is the inner sleeve: a jewel case, the catalog, a split sheet for attorneys, and "Open credits" for the roles he wants filled.

## How the asks read
- Representation: the hero's first button and Bonus track 01 lead to representation.html, which ends on a 3-step "Start a conversation" form.
- Investors: "Back one brand, or the whole universe" with two buttons, One brand and The universe.
- Unlock the showcase: orange button in header and hero, plus a Hidden track section with a pick-and-register form.
Media kits, Press, Case studies and Book sit in the footer.
