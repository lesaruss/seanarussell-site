# Blueprint: rationale

**Big idea.** The site is a drawing set, with Sean as the architect of a universe of brands. The pages use grid paper, numbered sheets, a title strip, callouts with leader lines, dimension lines, a legend, a door schedule and revision history. Light paper sheets alternate with ink "cyanotype" sheets. Orange marks only the core, the punch line and the next step. "From blur to blueprint" is the structure of the site, not just a tagline.

**How the asks read.** Sheet 06, "Entrances," is a floor plan with three doors and one ask per door:
- Door A, Representation, links to representation.html.
- Door B, Investors, offers one brand or the whole universe.
- Door C, Unlock the showcase, is orange and repeated in the header. The showcase sheet works: pick interests, register, see a thank-you.

On the representation page, "What I have" is Schedule A, "What I want" is three openings, and the form is a 3-step Form R-6 with Back and Next.
